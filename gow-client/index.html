      How to play

      Le Jeu de la Guerre · the rules as played here

      Goal

      Two armies, North and South, face each other on a 25×20 map. You win by destroying both enemy arsenals or eliminating every enemy fighting unit (infantry, cavalry and cannons). Who starts is drawn at random.

      Your turn

      • Move up to 5 different units, each once, by dragging them. A unit travels up to its movement in any of the 8 directions; it can't end on a mountain or an occupied square.
      • Attack once by dragging one of your connected combat units onto an enemy within its range. Relays can't attack.
      • Take an arsenal by moving a combat unit onto an enemy arsenal: it is destroyed.
      • Press End turn (or Space) to finish your turn. After your moves you may still attack; your turn ends after an attack (successful or not) or taking an arsenal.

      Communication

      • Arsenals send lines of communication in the 8 directions; relays standing on a line pass it on, extending the network.
      • Lines are blocked by mountains and enemy units, but go through mountain passes.
      • A unit off the network is cut off (shown greyed out): it can't move and doesn't count in combat. Keep your army connected, and try to cut the enemy's lines.

      Combat

      • Attack = the attack of every connected enemy unit lined up with the target (straight or 45° diagonal) and close enough to reach it with its range.
      • Defence = the defence of every connected friendly unit lined up with the target within its own range. A relay adds its own defence when it is the target.
      • Cavalry charge: +3 for each connected cavalry unit right next to the target or in an unbroken straight line of up to 4 cavalry leading to it (all of them count, even beyond their range). Not against a fortress or mountain pass, nor from a fortress.
      • Mountains block lines of fire. While you drag onto a target, the side panel shows both totals and the expected result.
      Attack ≤ DefenceRepelled, nothing happens
      Attack = Defence + 1The target must retreat one square at the start of its next turn; if it can't, it is removed
      Attack ≥ Defence + 2The target is destroyed
